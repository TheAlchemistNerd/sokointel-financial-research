# Appendix 02 — Court-record evidence, entity boundaries and open claims

### Companion to Project 3, Parts 11, 12 and 13

This is an evidence ledger, not a complete history of any retailer or startup. Court records can establish orders and can summarize reports or parties' submissions. A creditor's petition amount remains a claim unless adjudicated; a later administration report is not necessarily what a lender or supplier knew earlier. Keep the exact company name attached to each event.

## A2.1 Case record: what is established and what is not

| Case/entity | Dated record currently cited | What the cited record supports | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nakumatt Holdings Limited | High Court ruling of 11 March 2021 in Insolvency Cause 10 of 2017, citing an administrator's update dated 1 February 2021 [1] | The judgment says the administrator reported KSh 5,218,737,409 raised and more than KSh 3.5bn paid to creditors, including KSh 766,641,309 to landlords; the court extended administration for listed tasks | A complete audited pre-distress cash history, every asset disposal and cost, final recovery by class, or what a 2016 decision-maker knew |
| Tusker Mattresses Limited (trading as Tuskys) | Consolidated High Court judgment of 31 May 2023 [2] | Three petitioners claimed KSh 248,220,797.38, KSh 11,284,096.50 and KSh 30,892,518.92; the court found no demonstrated viable prospect of maintaining solvency and made liquidation orders described in the article | The company's total admitted liabilities, a full creditor waterfall, store-level contribution or the single cause of distress |
| Tusker Mattresses Limited | High Court ruling of 16 October 2025 [3] | A later proceeding names the company as in liquidation and discusses an interim liquidator's request for forensic audit and a statement of affairs | The final forensic findings, final asset realization, complete creditor recoveries or the earlier board's private information |
| Copia Kenya Limited | Kenya Gazette notice dated 31 May 2024 [4]; High Court ruling dated 23 April 2026 cited by a secondary case repository [5] | The official Gazette states administrators were appointed to Copia Kenya Limited; the later ruling is cited in the article as an extension/procedure update | Operating-company cash flows, the perimeter of every affiliate, investor recovery, or whether the company was a direct operating peer of Twiga |
| GT Flow Limited (formerly Twiga Foods One Limited) | September 2026 media reproduction of Gazette Notice No. 14595, cited as secondary [6] | The cited report attributes an administration appointment effective 17 August 2026 to the named legal entity | Direct verification of the Gazette page in this work; status of all companies using the Twiga brand; assets, liabilities or trading cash |
| Templar Field Limited (formerly Twiga Foods Limited) | September 2026 report citing a company release and Gazette notice, cited as secondary [7] | The report describes an administration appointment affecting this named company in September 2026 | The official notice and exact appointment date have not been directly verified here; it does not establish that subsidiaries are included |

The correct publication language for the last two rows is attribution: “the cited report states.” Obtain the official Gazette notices and administrator communications before upgrading the status to directly verified. Do not combine GT Flow and Templar Field into one company, or assume a group-wide order.

## A2.2 Arithmetic checks and claim labels

The three amounts cited in the Tuskys judgment add to KSh 290,397,412.80:

`248,220,797.38 + 11,284,096.50 + 30,892,518.92 = 290,397,412.80`.

This sum is an analyst calculation of three petitioning creditors' stated claims. It is not the total debt, the final admitted amount, the liquidation value or the loss to any class. Keep the source amounts individually visible in the evidence ledger so another reader can inspect the addition and the judicial context.

The Nakumatt figures are different in kind: “raised” and “paid” are statements summarized by the court from an administrator's update. Do not call the full amount raised gross sales, current cash or creditor recovery. The amount paid to landlords is a component of reported creditor payments and must not be added again to the total. The 2021 ruling is useful later process evidence; it is not a contemporaneous 2017 forecast.

## A2.3 Entity and funding ledger for the Twiga/Copia comparison

A round-by-round funding record should preserve one row per instrument and legal recipient:

| Field | Required treatment |
|---|---|
| Legal issuer and recipient | Use the registered company that signed or received the instrument; note trading and former names separately |
| Announcement, signing and cash dates | These are different events; do not treat a press announcement as cash in the bank |
| Amount and currency | Preserve original denomination; document FX rate, source and conversion date if aggregating |
| Instrument and seniority | Separate ordinary/preference equity, convertible note, secured debt, grant, supplier credit and customer advance |
| Status | Announced, committed, signed, closed, drawn, received, repaid, converted, written down or disputed |
| Use and restriction | Record permitted purpose, tranche conditions, shareholder/affiliate recipient and known restrictions |
| Evidence grade | Filing, signed agreement, investor record, company release, court/administrator material, press report or unresolved |
| Claim perimeter | Name the company whose assets and liabilities are legally in scope; map parent and subsidiaries separately |

A funding aggregate should only sum comparable cash actually received by the same defined perimeter over the same dates. If one report mixes equity rounds announced in USD, local-currency debt and investor commitments, display those as separate categories until transaction evidence supports conversion and inclusion. Fundraising is not revenue, cash remaining, enterprise value or investor loss.

## A2.4 What would permit a historical cash reconstruction

For Nakumatt and Tuskys, request audited statements, monthly management accounts, bank and mobile-money statements, store sales, stock ledger and counts, purchase ledger, supplier terms and confirmations, lease schedules, payroll and tax dues, debt schedules, board forecasts, asset register, administrator reports, statements of affairs and realization accounts. For each item, record whether it existed before the decision date, when it became available, which legal entity it concerns and whether it has been independently verified.

For Copia, GT Flow and Templar Field, add intercompany account reconciliations, inventory ownership, route-level contribution, customer-credit ageing, supplier claims, cash restrictions, funding agreements and the legal notices. Only after those records are reconciled should the analysis estimate company owner earnings, cash runway, creditor recovery or investor loss. Until then, the workbook's fictional operating schedules demonstrate calculations; they are not a substitute for historical company data.
