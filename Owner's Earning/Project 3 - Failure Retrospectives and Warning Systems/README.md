# Project 3 — Owner Earnings, Failure Retrospectives and Warning Systems

Parts 11–13; three planned articles, approximately 10,500 words. The project asks which cash, funding, governance, supplier and customer warnings were knowable before distress, using evidence graded by quality and date.

**Folder name:** Project 3 - Failure Retrospectives and Warning Systems

Period cash, normalized owner earnings and prudent distributions will be shown separately. The complete calculation and sector adjustments remain in the canonical programme.

## Part-by-part research and workbook breakdown

### Part 11 — The retrospective method

**Decision:** Was a warning visible at the time, or only after the outcome was known? **Research:** period owner earnings and burn; DSO, DIO, DPO and supplier arrears; unrestricted funding runway and debt claims; governance decisions; customer and supplier behaviour; alternative explanations. **Article path:** define an evidence hierarchy and contemporaneous-information cutoffs before any case. Build a common cash bridge, CCC table, funding register (separating announced commitments from disbursements; equity, debt and grants; legal obligor, currency, FX date, maturity and recoveries), decision timeline and counterparty map. Mark fact, management claim, secondary report, analyst estimate, hypothesis and missing data separately. “Insufficient evidence” is a legitimate conclusion. **Future workbook:** `P11 Evidence Ledger`, `P11 Timeline`, `P11 Cash and CCC Bridge`, `P11 Funding Runway`, `P11 Alternative Explanations`. **Evidence gate:** court, administrator, audited, regulator, contract and company material ahead of narrative reports; source cards are leads only.

### Part 12 — Nakumatt and Tuskys retail retrospectives

**Decision:** Did stock, supplier financing, expansion and governance make the retail cash cycle fragile before visible collapse? **Research:** dated store, inventory, payables, borrowing, capex and supplier-delivery records; contractual DPO versus arrears; management and governance events. **Article path:** separate company chronologies and bounded cash bridges, then a like-for-like comparison. Establish whether stop-supply, empty shelves, closures and governance decisions preceded or followed liquidity stress. Rate what a manager, supplier, lender and outside analyst could actually know at each date. **Future workbook:** `P12 Nakumatt Evidence`, `P12 Tuskys Evidence`, `P12 Retail CCC`, `P12 Timeline Comparison`. **Evidence gate:** supplemental claims about store counts, revenue, stock shortfalls and causation must be verified in primary materials; do not invent a precise historical owner-earnings total where records are incomplete.

### Part 13 — Twiga, a verified startup comparator and cross-case warnings

**Decision:** Did external capital cover a weak operating cash engine, and which warning signals could have prompted action before runway ran out? **Research:** Twiga's verified legal/operating status by date; inventory ownership, spoilage, customer credit, supplier terms, infrastructure, unit contribution, funding rounds and governance. Choose **Copia or Zumi** as the second startup case only after a documented primary-source quality screen; record why the other was not selected. **Article path:** dated case timelines, confidence-tagged cash/runway reconstructions and a comparative warning matrix across the startup cases, Nakumatt and Tuskys. Include owner earnings, contribution, DSO, DIO, DPO, overdue payables, debt service, runway, governance overrides, supplier response and customer collection; rate each signal's contemporaneous visibility, false-positive risk and practical response. September 2026 reporting identifies GT Flow Limited (formerly Twiga Foods One Limited) and Templar Field Limited (formerly Twiga Foods Limited) as under administration. Obtain and inspect the Kenya Gazette notice and administrator releases before publication; administration is not liquidation, and the ultimate outcome for creditors or wider operations remains unresolved. The related [startup-funding companion project](../What%20Startup%20Funding%20Figures%20Actually%20Measure/README.md) examines the market-level figures and the difference between funding raised and losses. **Future workbook:** `P13 Twiga Evidence`, `P13 Candidate Selection`, `P13 Startup Cash and Runway`, `P13 Supplier-Customer Map`, `P13 Cross-Case Warnings`. **Evidence gate:** direct confirmation of legal status, funding totals, exchange conversions and causal claims; if financial records are insufficient, publish bounded or qualitative findings.
