› Are the codes a complete description of the fundamental analyses included in the Buffett_Strategy_Research.rmd


• Not fully.

  - Gross margin moat proxy.
  - SG&A burden.
  - R&D intensity.
  - Long-term debt vs. net income.
  - CFO vs. net income cash conversion.
  - CapEx intensity.
  - SBC dilution burden.
  - Goodwill / assets.
  - Receivables and inventory growth spreads.
  - App Store fee-compression stress-test helper.

  It does not fully cover:

  - Qualitative moat scoring for network effects, switching costs, intangible assets, and cost advantages.
  - Antitrust architecture scoring beyond the simple App Store stress-test.
  - Black-Scholes / Merton comparison calculations.
  - Equity Premium Puzzle or myopic loss-aversion modeling.
  - Microsoft-style historical antitrust case logic.
  - Footnote-level maintenance CapEx extraction from 10-K text.
  - Segment-level breakup valuation.
  - Full peer comparison or multi-company ranking.

  So the code is complete as a Buffett red-flag scanner, but not complete as a full software implementation of the entire research framework.

  A better appendix would add separate modules/classes for:

  1. MoatScorecard
  2. RegulatoryRiskAuditor
  3. OwnerEarningsModel
  4. MertonComparisonModel
  5. SegmentBreakupStressTest
  6. PeerComparisonEngine
