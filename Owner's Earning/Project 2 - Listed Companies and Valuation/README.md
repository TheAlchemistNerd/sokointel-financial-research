# Project 2 — Owner Earnings for Listed Companies and Valuation

Parts 6–10; five planned articles, approximately 17,500 words. The project reconstructs company cash earning power and valuation ranges across US and NSE issuers, with methods adapted to asset-light businesses, asset-heavy businesses and regulated financial institutions.

**Folder name:** Project 2 - Listed Companies and Valuation

**Planned workbook filename:** Owner Earnings - Listed Company and Sector Valuation Workbook.xlsx (not yet created).

## How to use this folder

Status: planning only. The articles, workbook and calculators described here have not been built by this organisation step. This README is a project-specific view of the [canonical three-project, thirteen-part programme](../Canonical%20Research%20Programme%20-%20Three%20Projects%20and%20Thirteen%20Parts.md), which remains the master outline. Each article is planned at approximately 3,500 words, excluding references, appendices and workbook instructions. Worked article calculations will match the project's workbook. Editable user assumptions will be highlighted, sourced inputs kept distinct, formulas protected, and results recalculated when inputs change. Each workbook will include Guide, Sources, Assumptions, Checks, Change Log and Verification Cases sheets alongside the part-specific modules below.

The shared owner-earnings calculation begins with reconciled profit, adds justified non-cash charges, and deducts maintenance investment and required additional operating working capital. Owner compensation, taxes, debt claims, liquidity reserves and sector-specific capital needs require further treatment. Period cash, normalized owner earnings and prudent distributions will be shown separately. The complete calculation and sector adjustments remain in the canonical programme.

## Part-by-part research and workbook breakdown

### Part 6 — Reconstructing owner earnings from public filings

**Decision:** What dated per-share cash-earning range can an investor defend? **Research:** income/cash-flow/balance-sheet links, non-cash items, maintenance versus growth capex, working-capital versus financing movements, claim type and share-count change. **Article path:** rebuild multi-year filing history, normalize cycle and one-offs, show low/base/high owner earnings, then introduce equity value, enterprise-to-equity and reverse-DCF checks without claiming precision. **Future workbook:** `P6 Filing Inputs`, `P6 Owner-Earnings Bridge`, `P6 Claim and Share Check`, `P6 Valuation Range`. **Evidence gate:** audited annual reports and filings, exact notes/pages, and a dated price and share count.

### Part 7 — US technology and asset-light economics, anchored by NVIDIA

**Decision:** What remains for owners after physical and intangible productive capacity is maintained? **Research:** NVIDIA's reported cash bridge, cyclicality, inventory, supply commitments and customer concentration; R&D and capitalized software; SBC, dilution/buybacks and deferred obligations. **Article path:** a dated NVIDIA filing reconstruction, then a selected software/platform comparison showing different asset and revenue economics. Stress demand, gross margin, reinvestment, working capital and share count. NVIDIA is an anchor for future research, not a current valuation conclusion. **Future workbook:** `P7 NVIDIA Filing Bridge`, `P7 Intangible Reinvestment`, `P7 SBC and Dilution`, `P7 Scenario Valuation`. **Evidence gate:** current SEC and issuer filings before any numerical claim.

### Part 8 — Asset-heavy issuers and data-centre infrastructure

**Decision:** Which cash spending preserves existing service and which creates new capacity? **Research:** physical replacement cycles, power/cooling, real estate, leases, financing and construction-in-progress; unit economics of an established asset-heavy issuer and a selected data-centre infrastructure issuer. The latter is the second planned topical sector; whether it is “trending” and which issuers make sound comparators must be checked when research begins. **Article path:** reconcile total capex into maintenance, compliance and growth ranges; model debt/lease claims, utilisation and customer concentration. Add an energy-transition or regulated-utility sidebar to contrast capital intensity under different revenue protections. **Future workbook:** `P8 Asset Register`, `P8 Maintenance-Growth Split`, `P8 Lease and Debt Claims`, `P8 Capacity Scenario`. **Evidence gate:** audited filings, capex and lease notes, contracts where available and a documented comparator selection.

### Part 9 — Banks and insurers: distributable capital

**Decision:** What can a financial institution distribute while remaining able to lend, absorb losses and pay claims? **Research:** bank loan growth, expected credit losses, funding and regulatory capital; insurer underwriting, claims reserves, investments, asset-liability matching and solvency. Explain why ordinary inventory/CCC and physical-capex formulas cannot simply be transferred to either institution. **Article path:** a bank normalized-earnings/excess-capital/residual-income bridge and an insurer underwriting/reserving/solvency bridge, each with stress cases and payout limits. **Future workbook:** `P9 Bank Capital`, `P9 Credit Stress`, `P9 Insurance Underwriting`, `P9 Reserves and Solvency`, `P9 Distributable Capital`. **Evidence gate:** audited reports, regulator returns and current capital rules.

### Part 10 — NSE cross-sector casebook and decision framework

**Decision:** How should Kenyan issuers be compared without one formula flattening their economics? **Research scope:** actual dated cases across **NSE banks, insurance, energy, agriculture, telecommunications, manufacturing and property**. The bank and insurer methods extend Part 9; non-financial cases apply the correct replacement, lease, commodity, biological-asset, spectrum, network, capacity, property and fair-value adjustments. **Article path:** a source-linked bridge and low/base/high range per selected issuer, plus a cross-sector matrix of cash claim, adjustment sensitivity, governance, currency, state influence, liquidity and evidence quality. Compare the estimate with dividends and price at a defined date; no forced ranking by a single ratio. **Future workbook:** `P10 NSE Sources`, sector sheets, `P10 Cross-Sector Dashboard`, `P10 Decision Range`. **Evidence gate:** NSE/CMA, issuer and sector-regulator primary records; select actual issuers only after evidence sufficiency is assessed.
