# Project 2 technical appendices

These appendices provide reproducible calculations and sector-specific model structures beside Parts 6-10. They state which issuer data are filed, which values are derived, and where disclosures do not support a normalized estimate.

| Appendix | Focus | Article links | Workbook tabs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Filing-to-cash reconciliation, BAT Kenya arithmetic checks and equity-valuation gates | Parts 6 and 10 | Filing Inputs; NVDA Bridge; Valuation; Sources and Checks |
| 02 | NVIDIA FY2026 cash bridge, SBC treatment, working capital and an explicitly fictional infrastructure screen | Parts 7 and 8 | Filing Inputs; NVDA Bridge; Asset Heavy; Assumptions |
| 03 | Bank and insurer distributable-capital questions and a seven-issuer evidence map | Parts 9 and 10 | Bank Insurance; NSE Casebook; Sources and Checks |
| 04 | A normalized, profile-specific stress matrix for two US issuers and seven NSE industry cases | Parts 7–10 | Profile Stress; Filing Inputs; Asset Heavy; Bank Insurance; NSE Casebook |

The `Profile Stress` sheet applies transparent analyst-selected shocks to normalized indices; those outputs are not issuer forecasts, valuations or empirical risk estimates. The workbook remains a working version: it is not a multi-year owner-earnings model for every issuer and does not calculate Jubilee solvency or seven completed sector valuations. The appendices preserve those limitations.
