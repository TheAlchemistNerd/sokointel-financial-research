## Entity and outcome rules

1. Record a brand, parent, legal borrower, share issuer, operating company, asset owner and administrator as separate entities when they differ.
2. Treat announcement, signed commitment, closed round, drawdown and cleared cash receipt as different funding events.
3. Preserve instrument and original currency. Convert only with a dated exchange rate and show the source amount alongside the conversion.
4. Use dated labels such as operating, downsized, restructured, under administration, in liquidation, acquired, closed or unresolved. An administration appointment is not automatically a final liquidation or proof that operations ended everywhere.
5. Record debt recovery, equity proceeds, secondary sales and business-continuation value separately. Do not label all capital associated with an administration as “lost.”
6. Keep missing records as missing. The selected list is not a random or complete population and cannot establish a startup failure rate.
