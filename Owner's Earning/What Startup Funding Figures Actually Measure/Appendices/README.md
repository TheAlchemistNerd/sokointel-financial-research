# Startup funding research appendices

These appendices turn the source and scenario work behind the three articles into checkable calculations. The companion workbook is [Startup Funding Market and Outcomes Workbook.xlsx](../Startup%20Funding%20Market%20and%20Outcomes%20Workbook.xlsx). It preserves tracker-reported values, screenshot claims, analyst assumptions and derived arithmetic as separate evidence types.

| [02 — Instrument fit, debt service and equity dilution](Appendix%2002%20-%20Instrument%20Fit%20Debt%20Service%20and%20Equity%20Dilution.md) | What claims follow a hypothetical financing choice? | Part 02 | Instrument Guide; Debt Service; Equity Dilution |
| [03 — Thirteen-company claim and outcome register](Appendix%2003%20-%20Thirteen%20Company%20Claim%20and%20Outcome%20Register.md) | What does the supplied selected-company list establish? | Part 03 | Screenshot List; Company Outcomes; Deal Ledger; Sources and Checks |
| [04 — Provider comparison and denominator audit](Appendix%2004%20-%20Provider%20Comparison%20and%20Denominator%20Audit.md) | Could provider totals differ because of reporting dates, vintages or scope? | Part 01 | Provider Comparison; Reporting Vectors; Deal Ledger; Sources and Checks |

The workbook's loan and equity cases are explicit hypotheticals. The 13-company amounts are source-post claims and are not verified capital raised, company cash, investor loss or a representative startup cohort. The provider publication timeline records report dates, not underlying transaction cutoffs; those cutoffs and source vintages remain undisclosed in the inspected reports. Several legal-entity, transaction and outcome questions remain open; the appendices label those gaps rather than filling them with estimates.
