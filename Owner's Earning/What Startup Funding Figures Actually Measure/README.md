# What Startup Funding Figures Actually Measure

## Video guide

[Video Tutorial Guide.md](Video%20Tutorial%20Guide.md) maps three episodes to the provider-denominator audit, instrument examples, company outcome evidence, appendices and workbook.

This folder contains a completed three-article research draft and a companion workbook, developed from the user-supplied LinkedIn screenshots and tracker publications. The screenshot wording and funding amounts are retained as claims; they are not treated as audited transactions.

Each article exceeds 3,500 body words before references. Its technical appendix is linked in the article and indexed in [Appendices](Appendices/README.md).

## Companion workbook

[Startup Funding Market and Outcomes Workbook.xlsx](Startup%20Funding%20Market%20and%20Outcomes%20Workbook.xlsx) is built. `Market Metrics` compares matched H1 tracker totals; `Provider Comparison` keeps ATBD and Partech definitions, totals, instrument mixes and arithmetic side by side; `Reporting Vectors` records publication dates and makes the unknown transaction cutoffs, data vintages and year-assignment rules visible; `Funding Mix` retains the US$19m gap between the exact secondary-reported headline and debt-plus-equity subtotal; `Screenshot List` reproduces the 13 KES claims and selected-list arithmetic; `Deal Ledger` is an empty evidence-first transaction template with separate provider/report/first-seen/snapshot date fields; `Instrument Guide`, `Debt Service` and `Equity Dilution` support Part 2's hypothetical examples; `Company Outcomes` and `Sources and Checks` support Part 3. The workbook does not infer named-company loan terms, complete investor losses or a failure rate from the selected 13.

The reporting-vector sheet and source register now record that ATBD offers subscriber access to a monthly updated deal database, while also distinguishing a current export from the dated vintage needed to explain its January 2026 report. No ATBD transaction export was acquired, and Partech's published report includes confidential observations that cannot be recreated from public announcements alone.

## Publication status and open evidence

The articles and workbook are working research products, not a complete audit of Kenyan startup transactions. Before making company-level funding or recovery claims, reconcile original amounts, currencies, legal recipients, announced versus drawn funds, country allocation, and administrator or court records. The workbook's published market-level figures can be reproduced, while the underlying company deal ledger and many case outcomes remain unverified. Do not describe that evidence gap as a finding that the reported companies received no funding or that investors lost the screenshot amounts.

This series studies the **funding market**. The adjacent [Owner Earnings thirteen-part programme](../Canonical%20Research%20Programme%20-%20Three%20Projects%20and%20Thirteen%20Parts.md) studies **company-level cash earning power** and uses company histories in Parts 11–13. The two series link aggregate capital supply to entity-level cash and repayment evidence without assuming that market totals predict individual-company outcomes.
