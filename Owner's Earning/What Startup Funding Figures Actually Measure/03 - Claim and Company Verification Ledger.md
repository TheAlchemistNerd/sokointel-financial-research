## Twiga: precise update for the Owner Earnings plan

The planned research question now concerns reported administration as well as earlier restructuring. Source [8] reports a notice concerning **GT Flow Limited**, effective 17 August 2026 and published in the 11 September 2026 Kenya Gazette, and a September administrator communication concerning **Templar Field Limited**. These are **secondary-report details**, not a claim that this project has inspected the notice or press release.

Acquire the notice, confirm entity names and effective dates, obtain the administrator's appointment and subsequent communications, and distinguish trading status from legal procedure. The official Business Registration Service describes administration as an alternative to liquidation [9]. Administration alone does not establish final creditor recoveries, the value of all equity, or cessation of all related operations. This status distinction has been inserted in [Owner Earnings Part 13](../Canonical%20Research%20Programme%20-%20Three%20Projects%20and%20Thirteen%20Parts.md).

## Minimum records for the next research stage

| Record | Fields needed |
| --- | --- |
| Source | Source ID; publisher/issuer; document title; original URL or preserved file; publication and retrieval date; exact page/section; what it establishes; limitations and subsequent correction. |
| Entity | Legal entity name and identifier; former name; brand; parent/subsidiary relationship; domicile; operations; funded recipient and debtor; source supporting the relationship. |
| Deal | Stable deal ID; announcement date; commitment, close and drawdown dates where known; original amount and currency; instrument; tranches; lender/investor; recipient; security/recourse; maturity; use of proceeds; refinancing or duplicate flags. |
| Conversion | Original currency amount; FX rate, convention and date; conversion source; translated amount; nominal/real treatment. Keep the historical conversion distinct from a present-day translation. |
| Country allocation | Tracker and version; its eligibility/threshold rule; headquarters versus operations; multi-country handling; stated allocation weights; unallocated amounts. A mutually exclusive allocation should total 100% per deal. |
| Outcome | Exact entity; as-of date; trading status; legal procedure and effective date; primary document; liabilities/claims if evidenced; asset sale or restart; unresolved questions. Trading status and legal procedure need separate fields. |
| Operating evidence | Revenue and contribution definition; reporting period; customer receipts; supplier payments; capex; working capital; unrestricted cash; debt service; limitations. No inference of missing financials from the size of a raise. |
| Investor/creditor result | Capital actually paid in; loans outstanding; disposals/distributions; dated recovery information; residual value and estimation basis; unknowns. Never assume every claim has zero recovery. |

Use missing/unknown values rather than zeros when the information is absent. Preserve original source amounts and transformations. Maintain dated snapshots so later discoveries do not become facts supposedly known before a company's distress.
