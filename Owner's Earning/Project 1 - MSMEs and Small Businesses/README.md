This README follows the [canonical three-project, thirteen-part programme](../Canonical%20Research%20Programme%20-%20Three%20Projects%20and%20Thirteen%20Parts.md). Blue font marks editable example or user inputs; sources and formulas remain distinguishable. Overview explains the main path through the schedules, and Sources and Checks records citations and reconciliations. The `Evidence Vectors` sheet records the owner-earnings example, cash forecast, monthly working-capital schedule, invoice ledgers, stock example, maintenance register and debt screen with entity perimeter, amount/unit, period or dated observation, source status, worksheet locator and limits. Because these are invented planning cases, source/report/retrieval dates are marked not applicable rather than implied. A dedicated `Article Case` tab reproduces Part 1 and Appendix 01's KSh 990,000 workshop bridge and dated draw-headroom example; the `Owner Earnings` tab remains a separate larger-scale editable scenario. The workbook is a working model with visible, unprotected formulas; it does not include a change log.

The shared owner-earnings calculation begins with reconciled profit, adds justified non-cash charges, and deducts maintenance investment and required additional operating working capital. Owner compensation, taxes, debt claims, liquidity reserves and sector-specific capital needs require further treatment. Period cash, normalized owner earnings and prudent distributions will be shown separately. The complete calculation and sector adjustments remain in the canonical programme.

## Part-by-part research and workbook breakdown

### Part 1 — The cash an owner can actually claim

**Decision:** Why can an owner report a profit yet lack safe drawing capacity? **Research:** Buffett's original definition; profit versus CFO, EBITDA, FCF and SDE; which cash claim belongs to whom; what non-cash add-backs really mean. **Article path:** a labelled hypothetical profit/cash puzzle, the nine-step bridge, three distinct outputs (period cash, normalized owner earnings, prudent distribution), then a glossary of category errors. **Workbook modules:** `Article Case` reproduces the article's KSh 990,000 workshop bridge and dated draw-headroom calculation; `Owner Earnings` is a separate editable scenario; `Overview`, `Cash13W` and `Sources and Checks` show the broader cash forecast and reconciliation path. **Evidence gate:** the 1986 shareholder letter and applicable accounting standards.

### Part 2 — Maintenance capital and working capital

**Decision:** How much must remain in the business simply to preserve current operations? **Research:** maintenance versus growth capex; replacement cost versus depreciation; recurring versus seasonal working capital; treatment of customer advances and supplier arrears. **Article path:** asset register and classification, multiple maintenance-capex estimation methods, AR/inventory/AP bridge and low/base/high range. **Workbook modules:** `Maintenance`, `Working Capital`, `Receivables`, `Inventory` and `Payables` separate replacement planning from cash-cycle and ageing inputs. **Evidence gate:** use asset, invoice and contract records; the workbook contains illustrative inputs, not a company-specific low/base/high maintenance estimate.

### Part 3 — The daily MSME operating toolkit

**Decision:** Which cash shortfall, weak transaction, overdue invoice, stuck stock or supplier exposure demands action today? The dedicated article must include:

1. A **rolling 13-week cash forecast** of dated opening cash, receipts, payroll, suppliers, tax, rent, debt and closing cash, with the lowest-cash week and shortfall trigger.
2. **Gross/net burn and runway**, separating recurring, growth and one-off uses; zero or positive net burn must not produce a misleading runway number.
3. **Contribution margin and unit/revenue/cash break-even**, with explicit variable/fixed cost classification and debt/maintenance requirements where relevant.
4. **A/R aging and DSO**, using invoice due dates, credit sales, matched periods, disputed balances, concentration and contractual terms.
5. **Inventory aging, ABC classification, turnover, DIO, sales velocity, reorder points and holding costs**, with seasonality, spoilage, lead time and stock-outs visible.
6. **A/P aging and DPO**, supplier criticality and overdue status; `CCC = DSO + DIO - DPO` must be read with the underlying ledgers so distressed non-payment cannot masquerade as efficiency.
7. A dated action log testing the cash, margin and relationship effects of collection, discount, return, markdown, reorder or payment-term decisions.

**Workbook modules:** `Cash13W`, `Receivables`, `Inventory`, `Payables`, `Unit Economics` and `Action Log` calculate sample cash/runway, ageing, ABC, reorder and break-even measures. **Evidence gate:** replace fictional inputs with operational ledgers and contracts; the supplied 3:1 LTV:CAC, holding-cost and stock-age thresholds remain unverified leads, not default rules.

### Part 4 — Owner pay, reserves, growth and debt

**Decision:** Draw cash, preserve it, borrow, hire or invest? **Research:** safe distributions after tax, debt and reserve; incremental return after capital and working-capital needs; loan tenor versus cash cycle or asset life; downside timing. **Article path:** normalize owner pay, set a reserve, model debt/covenant headroom and compare a growth project under delayed-receipt and supplier-withdrawal stress. **Workbook modules:** `Owner Earnings`, `Cash13W` and `Debt and Growth` separate cash after principal/reserve from an illustrative year-one financing-cost screen. **Evidence gate:** use actual loan contracts, tax rules and owner-pay records before a real decision; the screen is not an amortization or covenant model.

### Part 5 — Buying, selling and succession

**Decision:** What cash earning power transfers when the founder leaves? **Research:** EBITDA/SDE/owner-earnings reconciliation, documented add-backs, replacement management cost, customer/supplier dependence and acquisition debt capacity. **Article path:** buyer/seller/lender transaction bridge and a valuation range tied to normalized working capital, debt and surplus cash. **Workbook module:** `Buy Sell and Succession` is an evidence and handover checklist used with the earnings, maintenance, working-capital and debt schedules; it does not calculate purchase price or key-person sensitivity. **Evidence gate:** document each adjustment; do not generalize a private-business multiple.
