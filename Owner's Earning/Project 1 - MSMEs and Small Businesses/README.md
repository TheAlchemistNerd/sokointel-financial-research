# Project 1 — Owner Earnings for MSMEs and Small Businesses

Parts 1–5; five planned articles, approximately 17,500 words. The project asks how an owner measures, protects, reinvests and eventually transfers a business cash engine across different industries and asset profiles.

**Folder name:** Project 1 - MSMEs and Small Businesses

**Planned workbook filename:** Owner Earnings - MSME Operating and Decision Workbook.xlsx (not yet created).

Editable user assumptions will be highlighted, sourced inputs kept distinct, formulas protected, and results recalculated when inputs change. Each workbook will include Guide, Sources, Assumptions, Checks, Change Log and Verification Cases sheets alongside the part-specific modules below.

The shared owner-earnings calculation begins with reconciled profit, adds justified non-cash charges, and deducts maintenance investment and required additional operating working capital. Owner compensation, taxes, debt claims, liquidity reserves and sector-specific capital needs require further treatment. Period cash, normalized owner earnings and prudent distributions will be shown separately. The complete calculation and sector adjustments remain in the canonical programme.

## Part-by-part research and workbook breakdown

### Part 1 — The cash an owner can actually claim

**Decision:** Why can an owner report a profit yet lack safe drawing capacity? **Research:** Buffett's original definition; profit versus CFO, EBITDA, FCF and SDE; which cash claim belongs to whom; what non-cash add-backs really mean. **Article path:** a labelled hypothetical profit/cash puzzle, the nine-step bridge, three distinct outputs (period cash, normalized owner earnings, prudent distribution), then a glossary of category errors. **Future workbook:** `P1 Profit-to-Cash`, with editable highlighted inputs, source cells, sign checks and debt/reserve warnings. **Evidence gate:** the 1986 shareholder letter and applicable accounting standards, not AI paraphrases.

### Part 2 — Maintenance capital and working capital

**Decision:** How much must remain in the business simply to preserve current operations? **Research:** maintenance versus growth capex; replacement cost versus depreciation; recurring versus seasonal working capital; treatment of customer advances and supplier arrears. **Article path:** asset register and classification, multiple maintenance-capex estimation methods, AR/inventory/AP bridge and low/base/high range. **Future workbook:** `P2 Asset Register`, `P2 Maintenance Capex`, `P2 Working Capital`, with total-capex and balance-change reconciliation. **Evidence gate:** asset, invoice and contract records for a real MSME; do not adopt a generic maintenance/depreciation percentage.

### Part 3 — The daily MSME operating toolkit

**Decision:** Which cash shortfall, weak transaction, overdue invoice, stuck stock or supplier exposure demands action today? The dedicated article must include:

1. A **rolling 13-week cash forecast** of dated opening cash, receipts, payroll, suppliers, tax, rent, debt and closing cash, with the lowest-cash week and shortfall trigger.
2. **Gross/net burn and runway**, separating recurring, growth and one-off uses; zero or positive net burn must not produce a misleading runway number.
3. **Contribution margin and unit/revenue/cash break-even**, with explicit variable/fixed cost classification and debt/maintenance requirements where relevant.
4. **A/R aging and DSO**, using invoice due dates, credit sales, matched periods, disputed balances, concentration and contractual terms.
5. **Inventory aging, ABC classification, turnover, DIO, sales velocity, reorder points and holding costs**, with seasonality, spoilage, lead time and stock-outs visible.
6. **A/P aging and DPO**, supplier criticality and overdue status; `CCC = DSO + DIO - DPO` must be read with the underlying ledgers so distressed non-payment cannot masquerade as efficiency.
7. A dated action log testing the cash, margin and relationship effects of collection, discount, return, markdown, reorder or payment-term decisions.

**Future workbook:** `P3 13-Week Cash`, `P3 Burn and Runway`, `P3 Unit Economics`, `P3 AR Aging`, `P3 Inventory Aging and ABC`, `P3 AP Aging and CCC`, `P3 Action Log`. **Evidence gate:** operational ledgers and contracts; the supplied 3:1 LTV:CAC, 20–30% holding-cost, 60–90-day dead-stock and stop-supply thresholds remain unverified leads, not default rules.

### Part 4 — Owner pay, reserves, growth and debt

**Decision:** Draw cash, preserve it, borrow, hire or invest? **Research:** safe distributions after tax, debt and reserve; incremental return after capital and working-capital needs; loan tenor versus cash cycle or asset life; downside timing. **Article path:** normalize owner pay, set a reserve, model debt/covenant headroom and compare a growth project under delayed-receipt and supplier-withdrawal stress. **Future workbook:** `P4 Distribution and Reserve`, `P4 Growth Case`, `P4 Debt Timing`. **Evidence gate:** relevant loan contracts, tax rules and owner-pay records before real examples.

### Part 5 — Buying, selling and succession

**Decision:** What cash earning power transfers when the founder leaves? **Research:** EBITDA/SDE/owner-earnings reconciliation, documented add-backs, replacement management cost, customer/supplier dependence and acquisition debt capacity. **Article path:** buyer/seller/lender transaction bridge and a valuation range tied to normalized working capital, debt and surplus cash. **Future workbook:** `P5 Normalizations`, `P5 Transaction Bridge`, `P5 Debt Capacity`, `P5 Key-Person Sensitivity`. **Evidence gate:** document each adjustment; do not generalize a private-business multiple.
