# Appendix 03 — Debt, growth and transfer of the owner’s claim

### Companion to Project 1, Parts 4 and 5

This appendix places owner pay, reserves, borrowing and a sale in one decision sequence. It does not prescribe a universal reserve size, borrowing multiple or private-business valuation multiple. Each depends on contracts, operating volatility and the quality of the evidence.

## A3.1 Owner pay, distribution and reinvestment are separate

An owner can be paid for work and can receive a return on ownership. If the owner works as general manager, technician and salesperson, the accounts should identify the compensation for each role where practical. For a buyer's earnings bridge, replace any below-market compensation with the cost of hiring qualified people to perform the work that must continue. The adjustment is the difference between the normalized replacement cost and the amount already charged to profit—not the full replacement wage again.

A reserve policy should name the risk it covers, owner, amount, trigger and review date. Test the policy against actual cash dates and a downside receipt case. Part 4's example starts with KSh 2.4m unrestricted cash and KSh 1.76m of listed four-week obligations, leaving KSh 640,000 before reserves. A KSh 450,000 reserve leaves KSh 190,000 of base-case headroom. If the KSh 500,000 buyer receipt is late and a further KSh 250,000 of inventory must be funded, the trough is KSh 390,000—KSh 60,000 below the chosen reserve. The receipt is not subtracted from today's cash if it was never included; the change is modeled on the future dates when the receipt and replenishment would otherwise occur. This is a timing stress, not a conclusion that the business is insolvent.

## A3.2 Match the borrowing test to the loan

A loan is a dated obligation and a financing source, not revenue. For each facility, keep principal, interest, fees, draw conditions, maturity, repayment schedule, collateral, covenants, eligible receivables and prepayment terms in a contract register. The business's own cash available for debt service can be framed as:

`Cash available for debt service = operating cash receipts − cash operating costs − cash taxes − required working-capital investment − necessary maintenance and compliance spending`.

Then, where the facility or lender defines the measure that way:

`DSCR = cash available for debt service ÷ scheduled principal and interest`.

Never assume this definition is the covenant definition; quote the actual agreement. If CFADS or cash available is below scheduled debt service in a downside, identify the date, shortfall and realistic remedy. A longer tenor can reduce the periodic payment but may increase total interest or leave a balloon; match tenor to the asset's useful life and cash generation without treating a projected resale as certain repayment.

Credit-rationing theory explains why lenders may reject a higher offered rate when borrower quality is difficult to distinguish and adverse selection or borrower incentives matter [1]. That is a reason to improve verifiable records and align the facility to a cash-generating use, not evidence that any specific Kenyan lender will approve a particular structure. A lender's spreadsheet should be reconciled to management's operating forecast, not substituted for it.

## A3.3 Compare a growth project with using less capital

A useful project case includes initial cash, phased capacity, incremental contribution, working capital, required replacement spending, tax, financing and the date cash returns. A simple screening ratio such as stabilized cash contribution divided by invested cash can be a first comparison, but it is not a project IRR or proof of value. Test the factors that determine the trough: customer uptake, price, input cost, collection delay, supplier deposits, commissioning date and asset reliability. Include a stop, defer or resize decision before sunk cost becomes the argument for finishing.

The article's KSh 190,000 provisional distribution is not automatically the best use of cash. Put three cases side by side: retain the cash as a reserve; fund the proposed project from equity; or borrow only the amount whose dated repayment can be supported by conservative incremental cash. Add the owner's personal risk tolerance and any contract or legal restriction separately. Do not count the same retained cash as a reserve, project equity and debt-service cover at once.

## A3.4 Buyer, seller and lender bridge

A buyer should reconstruct the earnings claim before agreeing a multiple. A minimal normalized-earnings bridge is:

| Line | Buyer’s question |
|---|---|
| Reported profit or cash flow | Which entity, period, accounting basis and audit scope? |
| Owner compensation | What work is performed, by whom, and what market replacement cost is evidenced? |
| One-off adjustments | Is the item truly nonrecurring, outside normal operations, and supported by an invoice or ledger? |
| Maintenance and compliance | What assets, licences and service obligations must continue after closing? |
| Working capital | What level and quality of stock, receivables, payables and customer advances is needed to trade? |
| Customer and supplier dependency | What cash flow is at risk if a contract, key person or relationship changes? |
| Financing and obligations | Which debt, leases, tax, litigation, guarantees or deferred payments transfer? |

If reported profit is `P`, supportable add-backs are `A`, incremental replacement cost is `L`, normalized maintenance is `M`, and normalized working-capital reinvestment is `W`, then a transparent operating estimate might begin `P + A − L − M − W`, with taxes and financing treated consistently for the selected valuation method. It should not mix an enterprise-level cash flow with an equity discount rate or subtract interest from earnings and also include debt in the enterprise-to-equity bridge without reconciling the method.

The sale-price bridge is separate from earnings:

`Cash to seller = equity consideration + permitted cash − debt-like items − transaction costs − tax − retained obligations − contingent payments not yet received`.

Purchase agreements should define the cash-free/debt-free convention, normalized working-capital peg, permitted leakage, inventory quality, receivable collectability, earn-out metric, control during the earn-out and dispute process. An earn-out reallocates uncertainty; it does not make uncertain cash certain. For the buyer, add the post-closing working-capital shortfall, immediate safety or replacement spending and transaction integration costs to the initial capital requirement.

## A3.5 Succession is an operating control

The continuity file should identify who can approve prices, move cash, access customer records, renew licences, operate equipment, order stock and resolve a supplier issue. For each essential responsibility, name a trained backup, access procedure and time-to-recover. Test the process during an owner's absence. A documented handover also reveals which earnings adjustments reflect the owner's personal labour and which reflect relationships that may not transfer.

Family-business research treats succession as a process with preparation, relationships and governance, rather than a single date or signature [2]. Evidence from Italian family firms reports context-specific links between succession and performance, not a transferable forecast for every private sale [3]. Use this literature to ask about continuity and incentives; use the target company's records to estimate transferable cash.
