### Proposed paragraph: cash timing

Put the payment dates beside the quoted cost. The business may earn a positive contribution from the order and still reach an instalment before the customer's money arrives. Show the lowest projected cash balance, what remains drawable under the facility and the date that availability expires. This makes the decision concrete: which structure allows the business to complete the order, pay its obligations and preserve its minimum operating cash?

### Proposed paragraph: the three perspectives

The same loan can be viewed from three desks. The owner asks whether it finances an activity that will generate enough cash on time. The lender asks whether interest and fees cover funding, administration and credit losses while supporting the capital committed. An investor in the lender asks how much of the resulting earnings must remain in the institution before a dividend can be paid. Following all three helps explain why affordable, useful credit also needs a sustainable funding and capital model.
