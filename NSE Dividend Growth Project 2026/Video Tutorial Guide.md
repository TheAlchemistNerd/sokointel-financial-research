## Purpose and format

This two-episode tutorial set turns the written project into a practical Kenyan-equities workflow. It should help a viewer move from a cash amount and a dated market price to a staged dividend-growth allocation, then to a research watchlist that improves future decisions without forcing a purchase today.

Each video is educational. It should identify the date and source of every market input, label assumptions, distinguish historical information from an investor decision, and avoid personal recommendations. Use the companion workbook for Excel-specific instructions. On screen, show the mathematical relationship and explain the result in plain language.

Use a 12 to 16 minute main episode, a clean 16:9 screen capture, and captions. Open with the practical question, show the calculation or research record, interpret the trade-off, and close by directing the viewer to the companion article and workbook.

**Viewer question:** How can KES 100,000 be staged through a three-month contribution plan while leaving room for transaction costs and whole-share constraints?

## Production and editorial checklist

1. Use sources and company results with an on-screen as-of date.
2. Obtain any permissions needed for charts, platform screens or third-party market data.
3. State assumptions beside any return, dividend, tax or transaction-cost calculation.
4. Do not display brokerage credentials, account numbers, personal portfolios or private client data.
5. Provide captions, a transcript and a concise description with the article link.
6. Review the final edit for number accuracy, accessible contrast, audio clarity and any claim that could sound like personalised advice.

## Publishing the episode in the blog

1. Publish the video on the official YouTube channel.
2. In Django Admin, open **Research articles** and select NSE-DG-01 or NSE-DG-02.
3. In the **Video** section, keep **Video planned** selected until the video is available. Paste the public YouTube watch, share, shorts or embed URL into **Video URL** once published.
4. Save the article. The article page will replace the preparation notice with a privacy-enhanced YouTube embed. The Watch page will also label it as available.
5. Check the public article on desktop and mobile, then add the YouTube link, article link and workbook reference to the video description.

The site accepts public YouTube URLs only. An invalid or non-YouTube link remains a preparation placeholder instead of embedding an untrusted site.
