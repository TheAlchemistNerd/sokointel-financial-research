**How the Option Wheel Strategy Works (Educational Overview)**

The Wheel is a three-step cyclical strategy for generating income via options premiums: \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.moomoo.com/us/learn/detail-options-wheel-strategy-117831-250138079)\]

1.  **Sell Cash-Secured Puts (CSPs):** The investor sells a put option on a stock they are willing to own at a specific "strike price" and collects a premium. The investor must have enough cash to buy 100 shares if the stock falls below that price by the expiration date.

    - *Outcome 1:* Stock price stays above the strike; the option expires worthless, the investor keeps the premium, and sells another CSP.

    - *Outcome 2:* Stock price falls below the strike; the investor is "assigned" the shares at the strike price, essentially buying them at a potential discount.

2.  **Own the Stock:** If assigned shares, the investor now owns 100 shares of the stock (per contract) and can start the next phase: selling calls.

3.  **Sell Covered Calls:** The investor sells a call option against their newly acquired shares, collecting another premium. This caps the potential upside gain but generates income.

    - *Outcome 1:* Stock price stays below the call strike; the option expires worthless, the investor keeps the premium, and sells another covered call.

    - *Outcome 2:* Stock price rises above the call strike; the shares are "called away" (sold) at the strike price, and the investor keeps the premiums collected from both the put and the call phase. The cycle can then restart by selling more CSPs. \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.schwab.com/learn/story/three-things-to-know-about-wheel-strategy), [<u>2</u>](https://www.ebc.com/forex/a-beginner-s-guide-to-the-wheel-strategy-options), [<u>3</u>](https://www.cabotwealth.com/daily/options-trading/income-producing-options-strategy), [<u>4</u>](https://www.moomoo.com/us/learn/detail-options-wheel-strategy-117831-250138079)\]

**Risks and Considerations**

While the Wheel can provide consistent income, it is not without risk: \[[<u>1</u>](https://likuyani.cdf.go.ke/virtual-library/5P8045/default.aspx/OptionTradingWheelStrategy.pdf)\]

- **Downside Risk:** The primary risk is a sharp, sustained decline in the underlying stock price after being assigned shares, resulting in a loss on the equity position that may not be fully offset by the options premiums.

- **Opportunity Cost:** If the stock price skyrockets after selling a covered call, the investor misses out on further gains beyond the strike price.

- **Requires Active Management:** This strategy requires monitoring positions, managing expiration dates, and making adjustments, which takes time and knowledge.

- **Capital Intensive:** Selling cash-secured puts requires tying up significant capital in the brokerage account as collateral. \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.schwab.com/learn/story/three-things-to-know-about-wheel-strategy), [<u>2</u>](https://www.captrader.com/en/blog/wheel-strategy/), [<u>3</u>](https://www.moomoo.com/us/learn/detail-options-wheel-strategy-117831-250138079)\]
