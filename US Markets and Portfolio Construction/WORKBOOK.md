# Authoritative US Markets Workbook

The canonical companion workbook is:

`US_Markets_Portfolio_Construction_Final_Workbook.xlsx`

It contains thirteen formula-driven worksheets: Guide, Cost of Ownership, NRA Tax, Diversification, Blended Expense Ratio, HHI Concentration, Matrix Variance, Options Wheel, Tax Calculators, Rebalancing, DCA versus Lump Sum, Sequence of Returns and Case Study.

The case study applies its linked blended expense ratio to the stated gross-return assumption before projecting monthly balances. Earlier root, five-part and pre-reconciliation output workbooks were retired because their scope was narrower or because repeated sensitivity rows had been stored as values rather than formulas. Their identity, SHA-256 hashes and replacement rationale are preserved in `WORKBOOK_LINEAGE.md`.
