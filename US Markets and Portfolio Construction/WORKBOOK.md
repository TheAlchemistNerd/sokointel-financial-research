# Authoritative US Markets Workbook

The canonical companion workbook is:

`US_Markets_Portfolio_Construction_Final_Workbook.xlsx`

It contains thirteen formula-driven worksheets arranged around the complete nine-part research progression:

1. `P01 Guide & Map`
2. `P02 Cost & Ownership`
3. `P03 Cross-Border Tax`
4. `P04 Diversification` and `P04 Blended Cost`
5. `P05 Core Concentration`
6. `P06 Portfolio Risk`
7. `P07 Options Sleeve` and `P07 Tax & Location`
8. `P08 Rebalancing`
9. `P09 DCA & Contributions`, `P09 Sequence Risk` and `P09 Full Case Study`

The guide explains how each model supports the corresponding article and provides the shared input, formula and output conventions. Parts with more than one worksheet contain distinct calculations rather than duplicate summaries.

The full case study applies its linked blended expense ratio to the stated gross-return assumption before projecting monthly balances. Earlier root, five-part and pre-reconciliation output workbooks were retired because their scope was narrower or because repeated sensitivity rows had been stored as values rather than formulas. Their identity, SHA-256 hashes and replacement rationale are preserved in `WORKBOOK_LINEAGE.md`.
