# References — Consolidated Source Register
### US Markets and Portfolio Construction (Five-Part Series)

This register lists every source cited anywhere across the five articles and five appendices, deduplicated by URL, in order of first appearance across the series. Each article and appendix numbers its own in-text citations independently, starting at [1] — the numbers below are for this consolidated register only and do not match the in-text bracket numbers inside any single article. Every entry was located through this project's own web research and, unless noted otherwise, was directly retrieved (searched and/or fetched) during that research rather than carried over from the source material supplied at the start of the project. "Scope" describes the specific claim(s) each source was used to support.

## A note on sources not used

Two categories of material that surfaced during research were deliberately excluded from citation. First, live, constantly changing figures (share prices, intraday premium/discount, day-of quotes) were noted where encountered but not cited as if they were stable facts, since a citation with an "Accessed" date attached to a number that changes by the minute would misrepresent how current that specific figure remains by the time a reader checks it — where a fund's price or exact same-day AUM appears in the series, it is explicitly framed as illustrative rather than as a durable fact. Second, several aggregator sites encountered during research (ETF screener and quote sites) were found, over the course of this project, to carry stale structural information — most notably several sites still describing QQQ as a Unit Investment Trust, and one still describing SOXX as tracking the PHLX Semiconductor Sector Index, months after both facts had changed. These sites are not cited as sources of record anywhere in the series precisely because this project's own research demonstrated their unreliability on exactly the kind of structural fact this series depends on; every structural claim in the series is instead traced to the fund issuer, the index provider, or a primary regulatory source wherever one exists.
