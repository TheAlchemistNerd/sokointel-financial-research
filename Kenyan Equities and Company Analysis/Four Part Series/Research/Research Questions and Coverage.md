# Research questions and coverage

1. How can a Kenyan investor compare ordinary dividends, special distributions and Treasury bills on a consistent after-tax cash-outlay basis?
2. Which statement relationships and sector-specific indicators explain the sustainability of earnings, distributions and reinvestment?
3. How can an investor estimate a plausible range of business values while making growth, required returns and capital requirements explicit?
4. How do allocation, concentration, dependence, implementation costs and liquidity combine to shape portfolio risk and income?
5. How do contributions, inflation, return sequences, crisis assumptions and withdrawals affect long-term purchasing power and the ability to meet a household goal?

The source manuscripts supply the thematic scope. Current factual claims are supported by the linked research sources; the articles stand on their own. The completed company cases use BAT Kenya, Equity Group Holdings and Jubilee Holdings with a dated source ledger. Company-specific projections are explicit teaching scenarios calibrated from the reported period. They are not issuer guidance, current market forecasts, target prices, or investment recommendations.
