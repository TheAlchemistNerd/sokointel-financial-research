how is paye calculated with KRA

9 April 2026

PAYE (Pay As You Earn) in Kenya is calculated by applying graduated income tax rates (10% to 35%) to an employee’s taxable pay, which includes basic salary, allowances, and benefits. The process involves deducting NSSF and allowable pension contributions, applying personal relief (Ksh 2,400/month), and remitting the tax by the 9th of the following month via iTax. \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/images/publications/Pay-As-You-Earn-Guide-2022-.pdf), [<u>2</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/helping-tax-payers/faqs/more-about-paye), [<u>3</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/business/companies-partnerships/companies-partnerships-pin-taxes/companies-partnerships-file-pay), [<u>4</u>](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=25VKtFPZoaY&t=1), [<u>5</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/blog/841-taxation-of-lump-sum-payments)\]

1.  **Determine Gross Taxable Pay:** Sum all cash payments (salary, wages, bonuses, commissions) and non-cash benefits exceeding Ksh 5,000 per month.

2.  **Deduct Allowable Deductions:** Subtract non-taxable items such as NSSF and approved mortgage interest/pension contributions.

3.  **Apply Monthly Tax Rates (Finance Act 2023):**

    - **First Ksh 24,000:** 10%

    - **Next Ksh 8,333:** 25%

    - **Next Ksh 467,667:** 30%

    - **Next Ksh 300,000:** 32.5%

    - **Above Ksh 800,000:** 35%

4.  **Apply Reliefs:** Deduct the Personal Relief of **Ksh 2,400** per month (or Ksh 28,800 annually) from the calculated tax.

5.  **Insurance Relief:** If applicable, 15% of insurance premiums (life, health, education) can be deducted, up to a maximum of Ksh 5,000 per month.

6.  **Housing Levy:** A 1.5% levy is deducted from the gross salary, which is a deduction before computing taxable pay. \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/individual/filing-paying/types-of-taxes/individual-income-tax), [<u>2</u>](https://www.payecalculator.co.ke/blog/take-home-pay-on-a-kes-100000-salary-in-kenya), [<u>3</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/images/publications/Pay-As-You-Earn-Guide-2022-.pdf), [<u>4</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/helping-tax-payers/faqs/more-about-paye), [<u>5</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/blog/841-taxation-of-lump-sum-payments)\]

- **Gross Pay:** Ksh 100,000

- **Less NSSF/Pension:** -Ksh 2,000 (Example)

- **Taxable Pay:** Ksh 98,000

**Key Considerations**

- **Employer Mandate:** Employers are required to register for PAYE, deduct the tax, file returns by the 9th, and pay the tax via the [<u>iTax portal</u>](https://itax.kra.go.ke/).

- **Non-Cash Benefits:** Benefits like company cars or low-interest loans are taxed based on KRA valuation guidelines.

- **Lump Sums:** Bonuses and retirement payments are taxed by aggregating them with annual income. \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=25VKtFPZoaY&t=1), [<u>2</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/blog/841-taxation-of-lump-sum-payments), [<u>3</u>](https://nation.africa/kenya/business/kra-retains-fringe-benefit-tax-low-interest-rates-3790266), [<u>4</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/images/publications/Pay-As-You-Earn-Guide-2022-.pdf), [<u>5</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/helping-tax-payers/faqs/more-about-paye)\]

should housing levy be non-taxable?

9 April 2026

Under current [<u>Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA)</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/public-notices/2157-amendments-to-paye-computation-pursuant-to-the-tax-laws-amendment-act,-2024) guidelines, the Affordable Housing Levy (AHL) is now an allowable deduction from gross salary before calculating PAYE. \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/component/kra_faq/faq/732), [<u>2</u>](https://sgb.ac.ke/understanding-kras-new-tax-relief-on-housing-levy-and-shif-contributions/)\]

This means the levy is effectively "non-taxable" in the sense that it reduces your taxable income, similar to NSSF and pension contributions. \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/public-notices/2157-amendments-to-paye-computation-pursuant-to-the-tax-laws-amendment-act,-2024), [<u>2</u>](https://sgb.ac.ke/understanding-kras-new-tax-relief-on-housing-levy-and-shif-contributions/)\]

**Current Tax Treatment of Housing Levy**

- **Deductibility**: Pursuant to the Tax Laws (Amendment) Act, 2024, the 1.5% employee contribution is deducted from gross pay *before* income tax is applied.

- **Impact on PAYE**: By treating it as a deductible expense, your taxable pay decreases, which slightly lowers the total PAYE you owe.

- **Cessation of Relief**: Because the levy is now a direct deduction from taxable income, the previous **Affordable Housing Relief** (which was 15% of the contribution) has been **discontinued** to avoid "double relief".

- **Employer Contribution**: The 1.5% matched by the employer is also a deductible business expense for the company under [<u>Section 15 of the Income Tax Act</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/public-notices/2099-collection-of-the-affordable-housing-levy-by-kenya-revenue-authority-2). \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.facebook.com/tuko.co.ke/posts/kra-announces-tax-relief-on-housing-levy-shif-for-salaried-kenyans/982999470638968/), [<u>2</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/public-notices/2099-collection-of-the-affordable-housing-levy-by-kenya-revenue-authority-2), [<u>3</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/public-notices/2099-collection-of-the-affordable-housing-levy-by-kenya-revenue-authority-2), [<u>4</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/public-notices/2157-amendments-to-paye-computation-pursuant-to-the-tax-laws-amendment-act,-2024), [<u>5</u>](https://sgb.ac.ke/understanding-kras-new-tax-relief-on-housing-levy-and-shif-contributions/)\]

**Why was this change made?**

Initially, the levy was deducted from *after-tax* income (gross salary without it being an allowable deduction), which many argued was a "tax on a tax". The 2024 amendment corrected this to align its treatment with other statutory social contributions like the [<u>Social Health Insurance Fund (SHIF)</u>](https://www.facebook.com/tuko.co.ke/posts/kra-announces-tax-relief-on-housing-levy-shif-for-salaried-kenyans/982999470638968/). \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.facebook.com/nation/posts/update-kra-says-housing-levy-will-be-deducted-from-gross-monthly-salary-which-in/675407877950366/), [<u>2</u>](https://sgb.ac.ke/understanding-kras-new-tax-relief-on-housing-levy-and-shif-contributions/), [<u>3</u>](https://www.facebook.com/tuko.co.ke/posts/kra-announces-tax-relief-on-housing-levy-shif-for-salaried-kenyans/982999470638968/)\]
