Instead of providing a generic cash income when you retire, a PRMF allows you to save funds **exclusively to cover your healthcare costs and medical insurance premiums** during your senior years. \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.kpapension.co.ke/products/post-retirement-medical-fund-prmf/), [<u>2</u>](https://citizen.digital/article/how-the-post-retirement-medical-fund-prmf-works-n359454), [<u>3</u>](https://www.kpapension.co.ke/products/post-retirement-medical-fund-prmf/)\]

**Tax Deductions & Reliefs for PRMF**

Contributing to an approved PRMF offers excellent tax advantages regulated by the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA): \[[<u>1</u>](https://smkadvocates.com/a-guide-to-the-public-benefits-organisations-pbo-regulations-2026/), [<u>2</u>](https://kppf.co.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/KPPF-Pension-Pulse.pdf), [<u>3</u>](https://www.liberty.co.ke/Products/boresha-maisha-individual-pension-plan1)\]

- **Insurance Tax Relief:** You receive a **15% tax relief** on the amount of premium/contribution paid into the PRMF, capped at a maximum of **KES 5,000 per month** (or KES 60,000 annually). \[[<u>1</u>](https://kppf.co.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/KPPF_POST_RETIREMENT_MEDICAL_FUND_MEMBER_BOOKLET.pdf)\]

- **Tax-Exempt Investment Returns:** The interest and returns earned from your invested contributions accumulate **100% tax-free**. \[[<u>1</u>](https://kppf.co.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/KPPF_POST_RETIREMENT_MEDICAL_FUND_MEMBER_BOOKLET.pdf)\]

- **Tax-Free Payouts:** When you retire, any funds transferred directly from the PRMF to a medical insurance provider to pay for your healthcare cover are **fully exempt from tax**. \[[<u>1</u>](https://kppf.co.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/KPPF_POST_RETIREMENT_MEDICAL_FUND_MEMBER_BOOKLET.pdf)\]

- **Setup:** A PRMF is typically established as an additional, optional fund **within an existing registered retirement benefits scheme** (either through your employer or an individual retirement plan). \[[<u>1</u>](https://citizen.digital/article/how-the-post-retirement-medical-fund-prmf-works-n359454)\]

- **Standalone Option:** Following recent National Treasury reforms, workers can also opt to save through standalone medical funds without necessarily being forced to join the main sponsoring medical scheme. \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/markets/post-retirement-medical-fund-savings-hit-sh135bn-on-reforms-4994090)\]

- **At Retirement:** While your standard pension pays for your daily food, housing, and utility bills, your PRMF balance is used to buy an annuity or medical cover so that expensive senior healthcare doesn't wipe out your lifestyle savings. \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.gdc.co.ke/blog/a-penny-more-a-day-secures-your-future-by-irene-moinkett/), [<u>2</u>](https://www.kpapension.co.ke/products/post-retirement-medical-fund-prmf/), [<u>3</u>](https://tscrbs.or.ke/index.php/our-funds/post-retirement-medical-fund), [<u>4</u>](https://www.kpapension.co.ke/products/post-retirement-medical-fund-prmf/)\]

**Private health insurance** policies in Kenya qualify for [<u>Insurance Tax Relief</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/blog/785-tax-reliefs-in-kenya-personal-and-insurance-reliefs), allowing you to claim back a percentage of your premium to directly reduce your PAYE tax liability. Unlike pension plans, which are fully tax-deductible from gross income, private health insurance works as a *tax credit* against the actual tax due. \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/blog/785-tax-reliefs-in-kenya-personal-and-insurance-reliefs), [<u>2</u>](https://www.payecalculator.co.ke/blog/how-insurance-relief-works-for-kenyan-taxpayers)\]

**How Health Insurance Tax Relief Works**

- **The Relief Rate:** You are entitled to a **15% tax relief** on the monthly premiums you pay for your private health insurance cover. \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/individual/filing-paying/types-of-taxes/individual-income-tax), [<u>2</u>](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/individual/other-tax-credits-and-incentives)\]

- **The Shared Cap:** The maximum overall insurance relief allowed by the [<u>Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA)</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/public-notices/1602-insurance-relief-on-contributions-to-national-hospital-insurance-fund-nhif) is **KES 5,000 per month** (or KES 60,000 annually). \[[<u>1</u>](https://ke.andersen.com/news/shif-contributions-eligible-for-15-tax-relief/), [<u>2</u>](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/individual/other-tax-credits-and-incentives)\]

- **The Fine Print:** This cap is **shared** across your private medical insurance, your statutory [<u>Social Health Insurance Fund (SHIF)</u>](https://ke.andersen.com/news/shif-contributions-eligible-for-15-tax-relief/) deductions, and any qualifying life or education policies you hold. \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.payecalculator.co.ke/blog/how-insurance-relief-works-for-kenyan-taxpayers), [<u>2</u>](https://ke.andersen.com/news/shif-contributions-eligible-for-15-tax-relief/)\]

**A Quick Example Calculation**

1.  **Total eligible premiums:** KES 12,000

2.  **Calculate 15% relief:** KES 12,000 × 15% = **KES 1,800**

3.  **Your Benefit:** KES 1,800 is subtracted directly from your calculated PAYE bill for that month, meaning you pay KES 1,800 less in tax. \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/blog/785-tax-reliefs-in-kenya-personal-and-insurance-reliefs), [<u>2</u>](https://www.payecalculator.co.ke/blog/how-insurance-relief-works-for-kenyan-taxpayers)\]
