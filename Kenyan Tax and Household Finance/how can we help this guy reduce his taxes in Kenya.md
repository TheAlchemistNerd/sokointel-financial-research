The tax reliefs and deductions you mentioned serve as highly effective tools to minimize individual income tax liability in Kenya. \[[<u>1</u>](https://rivermate.com/de/leitfaden/kenia/steuern)\]

The **Tax Laws (Amendment) Act** introduced changes to how these are structured, shifting some from post-tax reliefs to pre-tax allowable deductions. The specific rules and limits for each item when filing with the [<u>Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA)</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/) are outlined below: \[[<u>1</u>](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/individual/other-tax-credits-and-incentives)\]

**💼 Registered Pension Scheme**

Contributions to a registered pension, provident, or individual retirement fund act as an **allowable deduction**, meaning they reduce taxable gross income before tax is calculated. \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/component/kra_faq/faq/732), [<u>2</u>](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/individual/deductions), [<u>3</u>](https://faidihr.com/blog/how-affordable-housing-levy-affects-paye-in-kenya), [<u>4</u>](https://rivermate.com/de/leitfaden/kenia/steuern), [<u>5</u>](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/ng/pdf/provisions-of-the-finance-act-2021-relating-to-personal-income-tax.pdf)\]

- **The Tax Benefit:** The deduction reduces the amount of income subject to PAYE. \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.jify.co/blog/impact-of-government-financial-policies-on-your-salary/)\]

- **The Limits:** Tax exemption applies to the lower of:

  - Your **actual contribution** amount.

  - **30%** of your pensionable income.

  - A statutory cap of **KES 30,000 per month** (KES 360,000 per year). \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.pkfea.com/media/k00pnmya/quick-tax-guide-2023-2024.pdf), [<u>2</u>](https://www.gainsuranceltd.com/life/why-personal-pension-schemes-in-kenya/), [<u>3</u>](https://www.icpak.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Taxation-of-employee-emoluments-by-CPA-Karaya-Mokaya.pdf), [<u>4</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/component/kra_faq/faq/732)\]

**🏥 Post-Retirement Medical Fund (PMRF) \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.facebook.com/kbcradiotaifa/videos/-thinking-about-your-future-start-now-and-save-more-with-less-taxwhen-you-save-t/1424966471879934/)\]**

The treatment of PMRF contributions has shifted from a post-tax credit to a pre-tax benefit. \[[<u>1</u>](https://ieakenya.or.ke/blog/how-the-finance-bill-2025-could-lower-net-salaries-in-kenya/), [<u>2</u>](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/individual/other-tax-credits-and-incentives)\]

- **The Tax Benefit:** Contributions made into a registered PMRF are now categorized as an **allowable deduction** from gross income. This replaces the previous 15% insurance relief system to provide a more aggressive tax shelter for medical savings.

- **The Limit:** Deductions are allowed up to **KES 15,000 per month**. \[[<u>1</u>](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/individual/other-tax-credits-and-incentives), [<u>2</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/component/kra_faq/faq/732), [<u>3</u>](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/individual/other-tax-credits-and-incentives)\]

**🩺 Private Health Insurance**

Private health insurance policies qualify for **Insurance Relief**, which functions as a direct cash discount subtracted from your final tax bill rather than your gross salary. \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/individual/filing-paying/types-of-taxes/individual-income-tax), [<u>2</u>](https://woodruff-fp.co.uk/planning-your-retirement-guide/), [<u>3</u>](https://alphacap.co.ke/individual-income-tax-reduction-kenya/), [<u>4</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/component/kra_faq/faq/733), [<u>5</u>](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/individual/other-tax-credits-and-incentives)\]

- **The Tax Benefit:** A relief calculated at **15% of the premiums paid** for yourself, your spouse, or your children.

- **The Limit:** The relief is capped at a maximum of **KES 5,000 per month** (KES 60,000 per year).

- **Crucial Condition:** To qualify for tax relief, the health insurance policy must have a maturity period of **at least 10 years**. Short-term, year-to-year medical covers generally do not qualify unless bundled under specific eligible life or long-term products. \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/images/publications/PAYE-AS-YOU-EARN-PAYE_4-01-2025.pdf), [<u>2</u>](https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/blog/785-tax-reliefs-in-kenya-personal-and-insurance-reliefs), [<u>3</u>](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/individual/other-tax-credits-and-incentives)\]
