Their core tax advantage stems from the **"tax-neutral" gross roll-up regime**, meaning the fund itself pays zero Irish tax on its underlying income or capital gains. Tax liabilities are determined entirely by the individual investor’s jurisdiction and class of shares. \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.dilloneustace.com/download/1/Taxation%20of%20Irish%20Collective%20Investment%20Funds%20and%20Availability%20of%20Treaty%20Benefits%20In%20Press.pdf), [<u>2</u>](https://www.algoodbody.com/media/InFocus-Taxation-Irish-regulated-funds.pdf), [<u>3</u>](https://www.ogier.com/news-and-insights/insights/ucits-in-ireland-a-practical-guide-for-asset-managers/), [<u>4</u>](https://www.revenue.ie/en/companies-and-charities/financial-services/collective-investment-vehicles/funds.aspx)\]

**1. In Situ Taxation & Fund-Level Exemptions**

In situ (in-fund) taxation refers to how assets are treated within the UCITS wrapper itself.

- **Zero Irish Fund Tax:** The UCITS is fully exempt from Irish capital gains and corporate taxes on profits generated from underlying investments.

- **No Irish Stamp Duty:** The issue, transfer, or redemption of UCITS shares attracts zero Irish stamp duty.

- **VAT Exemption:** Management and administrative services provided to the UCITS are typically VAT exempt. \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.ogier.com/news-and-insights/insights/ucits-in-ireland-a-practical-guide-for-asset-managers/)\]

The biggest localized tax advantage of an Irish UCITS is treaty access, particularly regarding US equities. \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/institutional/insights/considerations-for-non-us-investors-us-etfs-vs-irish-ucits), [<u>2</u>](https://www.ogier.com/news-and-insights/insights/ucits-in-ireland-a-practical-guide-for-asset-managers/)\]

- **US-Ireland Double Tax Treaty:** Irish-domiciled UCITS ETFs benefit from a reduced **15% US withholding tax rate** on dividends.

- **Comparison to Direct US Funds:** Non-US residents holding US-domiciled ETFs directly are typically subject to a 30% dividend withholding tax.

- **Tax Neutrality:** Ireland itself does not impose any additional withholding tax on distributions to non-resident investors. \[[<u>1</u>](https://www.bogleheads.org/wiki/Nonresident_alien_investors_and_Ireland_domiciled_ETFs), [<u>2</u>](https://bratucapital.com/ucits-funds), [<u>3</u>](https://white-label.hanetf.com/insights/us-etfs-are-not-the-only-etf-wrapper-with-a-tax-advantage-irish-domiciled-etfs-have-one-too/), [<u>4</u>](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/institutional/insights/considerations-for-non-us-investors-us-etfs-vs-irish-ucits)\]

  - **Mechanism:** The fund automatically reinvests all dividends and interest internally back into the portfolio.

  - **Tax Implications:** Instead of receiving cash, the value of your shares simply appreciates. This frequently defers tax events until you officially sell the fund, allowing for a compounding effect.
