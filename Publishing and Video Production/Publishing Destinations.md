## Destination choices

| Platform | Intended use | Companion material |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Full article or an edition of an existing newsletter; a separate short introduction can invite discussion | Selected chart and a public link to the PDF, appendix or workbook |
| Substack | Full article in a series, with the option to distribute it by email | Downloadable reading edition and linked supporting models |
| Medium | Full story, retaining diagrams, citations and references | Canonical link to the original publication when cross-posting |
| Facebook | African Markets and Business Credit adaptations and the Leadership and Governance post: a self-contained explanation, selected visual and discussion question | Canonical Sokointel article link and supporting evidence |
| YouTube | Thirteen planned episodes built from the research-question storyboards | Deck handout, article, source register, captions and supporting models |

LinkedIn supports individual articles and newsletter editions. Substack offers web publication and email/app distribution choices. Medium’s import tool supplies a canonical link to the original article; its author can also set that link manually. Facebook supports text and photo/video updates. These platform functions are documented in the official sources below. The lengths and sequencing proposed here are editorial choices.

## Publication sequence

Choose one original home for each article, then retain that URL in the posting log. A useful default is to publish the long-form article on Substack, adapt its opening for LinkedIn, and import or cross-post it to Medium with the original URL recorded. Keep each project in part order. Prepare Facebook adaptations alongside the two African markets articles, and schedule the corresponding YouTube episode when its deck and recording are complete. This is a production sequence, not an automatic posting schedule.

For LinkedIn, write a short introduction that starts with the business or household decision and points to the full article. For Substack, retain the complete series context and link adjacent parts. For Medium, use clear subheadings and place the original-publication link through the canonical setting. For Facebook, develop one useful idea per post rather than compressing an entire research series into a teaser. All platform drafts should retain the period, unit and assumption when they quote a numerical finding.

The current package is prepared for local review and reuse. Publication destinations are assignments, not records of posts already sent. Enter the actual publication URL and date in the posting log when each article goes live.
