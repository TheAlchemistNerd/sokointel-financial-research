# International Investing and Diaspora Finance

Kenyan investors and diaspora households who want to understand international funds, relocation and dependable family transfers.

## Research questions

These questions preserve the existing series research agenda.

1. **IIDF-Q1** What does an investor actually own when choosing an ETF’s exposure, domicile, share class, and trading currency?
2. **IIDF-Q2** When does an Irish UCITS structure improve results after withholding, fund charges, and transaction costs?
3. **IIDF-Q3** How do residence and relocation affect taxable income, gains, wrappers, and reporting?
4. **IIDF-Q4** Which IBKR, Wise, bank, and remittance routes work for the actual customer and currency?
5. **IIDF-Q5** How can investing and dependable family support share a workable household plan?
