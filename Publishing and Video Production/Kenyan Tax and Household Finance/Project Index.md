# Kenyan Tax and Household Finance

Kenyan employees, families and self-employed viewers who want to connect their payslip, pension, healthcare and housing choices.

## Research questions

These questions index the research already developed in the four household-finance manuscripts.

1. **KTHF-Q1** How do PAYE, statutory deductions and actual household commitments determine spendable cash?
2. **KTHF-Q2** How do pension deductions, contributions, fees, inflation and investment paths affect retirement accumulation?
3. **KTHF-Q3** How can medical cover and accessible reserves work together when claims and income interruption occur?
4. **KTHF-Q4** How do mortgage rates, amortisation, prepayments and ownership costs compare with renting?
5. **KTHF-Q5** Which scenarios and records help a household adapt its financial plan as income, policy and costs change?
