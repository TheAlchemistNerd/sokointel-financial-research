Audience: Kenyan investors who want to understand company accounts and build portfolios they can explain, compare and maintain.

Case: Seven dated dividend examples, fictional company models and three educational allocation policies.

## Research agenda

1. **KECA-Q1** How can a Kenyan investor compare ordinary dividends, special distributions and Treasury bills on a consistent after-tax cash-outlay basis?
2. **KECA-Q2** Which statement relationships and sector-specific indicators explain the sustainability of earnings, distributions and reinvestment?
3. **KECA-Q3** How can an investor estimate a plausible range of business values while making growth, required returns and capital requirements explicit?
4. **KECA-Q4** How do allocation, concentration, dependence, implementation costs and liquidity combine to shape portfolio risk and income?
5. **KECA-Q5** How do contributions, inflation, return sequences, crisis assumptions and withdrawals affect long-term purchasing power and the ability to meet a household goal?
