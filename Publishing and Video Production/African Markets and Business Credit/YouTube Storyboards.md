Audience: Kenyan and regional business owners, working professionals, investors, and viewers curious about how regional capital reaches ordinary businesses.

Case: A Nairobi packaging business considering a larger order from Uganda.

## Research agenda

1. **AMBC-Q1** What does verified investment activity reveal about Kenya’s appeal to African infrastructure investors and banking groups?
2. **AMBC-Q2** Under what conditions can regional energy and transport infrastructure improve trade, business productivity, and currency resilience?
3. **AMBC-Q3** How do bank ownership, funding, credit quality, and Kenya’s loan-pricing reforms affect SME access to finance?
4. **AMBC-Q4** When does fintech credit help businesses grow, and how do its costs and repayment terms affect financial stress?
5. **AMBC-Q5** What should investors, business owners, and policymakers measure to determine whether financial expansion produces broader economic benefits?
